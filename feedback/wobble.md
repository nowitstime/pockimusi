# Wobble — feedback

The D-pad sets how the tone moves in time (PHASE_L.md L9). `B` root, `Y` 3rd, `X` 5th, `A` octave over track C's chord (a new note while one is held glides); the D-pad picks the wobble rate (`↑` 1/4, `↗` 1/8, `→` 1/16, `↘` 1/8T, `↓` closed, `↙` 1/2, `←` 1 bar, `↖` 1/16T; neutral keeps it); `L1` / `R1` octave, both = OPEN. The tone is the patch's bright layer (and a low-pass with EFX), so only patches with a bright layer are offered.

## Questions for the pass

1. Does rocking the D-pad while holding make the bass talk?
2. Are eight rates too many to remember? Which ones get used?
3. `SINE` (closed on the beat) or `SAW` (opens on the beat): which should be the default?
4. Is the movement smooth enough at 1/16T on the device (about 3–4 frames per wobble at 174)?
5. With EFX, does the low-pass add to it, or is the bright layer alone better?
6. Are the notes on the face buttons (root, 3rd, 5th, octave) the right four? Would the 7th or the 5th below be more useful than the 3rd?
7. Which patches wobble best? (`NEURO`, `REESE`, `DRONE`, `CHOIR` ...)
