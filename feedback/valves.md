## How it plays

| Control | Job |
| :-- | :-- |
| D-pad | The open note, a tone of the current chord: `↓` 5th below, neutral root, `←` 3rd, `↑` 5th, `→` 7th, `↘` octave |
| Face buttons | Valves lower the open note: `A` 1 · `B` 2 · `A+B` or `X` 3 · `A+X` 4 · `Y` 5 · all four 6 |
| `R1` | Blow: the note sounds while it is held |
| `L1` | Tongue: a second, softer breath (75 %) |
| A change while blowing | Slur (no new attack) |
| The other shoulder | A new attack on the same note (tonguing) |
| Rows | `PATCH` (opens on `SAW`), `VALVES` (`SCALE` / `CHROMATIC` steps), `REGISTER`, `FOLLOW` |

Nothing sounds without breath.

### Open from the plan
