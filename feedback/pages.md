# Pages — feedback

**What:** everything that isn't an instrument: the Track page, Clock, Mix, the stage and tape deck, recording · **Spec:** SPEC §3.4–3.6, §4, §11 · **Background:** FUTURE_PLANS §2–§5 · **Index:** [feedback/](README.md)

---

## Track page (hold `SELECT`)

Sound rows, settings, instrument tabs and effects for the active track.

- [ ] Do the ten instrument tabs read clearly as a carousel? Does the slide show which way I stepped?
- [ ] Does the fade at the strip's edges read on the device's screen, or does it need arrows?
- [ ] Can I find a sound quickly with ten instruments' worth of patches in each list?
- [ ] Is the instrument swap (preview, apply on release, undo) clear?
- [ ] Are the setting rows understandable without the docs?

## Clock (hold `START`)

Tempo, key, scale, bars, grid and swing.

- [ ] Can I set up a loop (tempo, key, bars) quickly at the start of a session?
- [ ] Is per-track grid and swing used? Is it clear which track it applies to?

## Mix (`SELECT + R2`)

- [ ] Can I balance four tracks quickly? Are the meters useful?
- [ ] Do the punch-in effects on `R2` get used? Should they be recordable?

## Recording

- [ ] Is recording (latch / hold on `L2`) clear to a new player?
- [ ] Undo, erase and overdub: do they do what I expect?
- [ ] Replace vs overdub, auto-stop after one pass (SPEC §4): wanted?

## The stage and tape deck

- [ ] On each instrument's screen, can I see what I'm playing at a glance?
- [ ] Do the labels on the controls match what they do?
- [ ] Does the tape deck show the loop clearly (notes, bends, chords)?

## Lab tool: `FINGERS`

Hidden unless `Config.LAB` is on. Shows the fingering the app sees.

- [ ] Do pairs and all four land cleanly?
- [ ] Is the 35 ms settle window right on the RG35XX's buttons, or do pairs need longer?
