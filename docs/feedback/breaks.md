# Breaks — feedback

Famous breaks rearranged live (PHASE_L.md L8, the patterns are in `src/breaks.lua`). Hold `B` to play in time; a D-pad direction picks a slice (1–8, clockwise from up) and jumps at the next 1/8 line; `A` stutters, `Y` plays backwards, `X` chokes; `L1` / `R1` half / double time; both = a snare roll into the bar line. Recording keeps your moves, so `BREAK` and `KIT` can be changed afterwards.

The breaks were first transcribed by ear onto a 1/16 grid before a listen on the device; they have been played since. Note any wrong hits below in the pass.

## Questions for the pass

1. Does each break sound like itself? Note the wrong hits per break (`AMEN`, `THINK`, `FUNKY DRUMMER`, `APACHE`, `HOT PANTS`, `COLD SWEAT`).
2. Can you play a recognisable jungle chop on `AMEN` within two minutes?
3. Jumps wait for the next 1/8 line. Too slow, or right? Would 1/16 feel better?
4. Stutter repeats one step of the track's grid (1/16 by default). Is that the right size?
5. Is the snare roll's speed-up (1/8 → 1/16 → 1/32) right, and should the break keep playing under it?
6. Which kit suits which break? Is `JUNGLE`'s higher snare right?
7. Does `X` (choke) want to be momentary (as now) or a tap?
8. Should a break be able to latch (keep playing without holding `B`)?
